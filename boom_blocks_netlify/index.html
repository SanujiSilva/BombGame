🎮 How to play

🕵️ Each player secretly selects one tile as their bomb.
✅ A safe tile gives the player who picked it +1 point.
💣 Tap another player's secret bomb: its owner gets +2 points, then play pauses so everyone can see the blast.
The bomb tile explodes and the player who picked it is marked as blasted for everyone. The blast plays when sound is on.
🏆 Keep picking until one secret bomb remains. The highest score wins the match.
🔊 Sound and music can be switched on/off during play.
